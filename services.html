What we do for you

Our services are tailored for Saudi companies that want high-quality German technology without the communication gaps and uncertainty. We stay by your side from first contact until the cooperation is running smoothly.

Representation in front of German manufacturers

We act as your point of contact in Germany. We present your company and projects to suitable manufacturers and make sure both sides understand each other – technically and commercially.

  • Present your company profile and applications
  • Introduce you to relevant German OEMs
  • Support structured follow-up and clarifications

Supplier scouting & shortlists

Instead of searching the internet for days, you receive a curated shortlist of manufacturers that match your technical, quality and delivery expectations.

  • Market screening in Germany and Europe
  • Check certifications, references and experience
  • Clear overview of strengths for each manufacturer

Commercial mediation & deal support

We facilitate communication between your team and the manufacturer. Final commercial and legal decisions always remain between you and the supplier.

  • Clarifying technical and commercial questions
  • Coordinating revised offers and options
  • Helping both sides reach a clear, workable understanding

Procurement & RFQ support

From RFQ to purchase order, we keep the process structured and transparent, especially when you are comparing multiple German offers.

  • Preparing and sending structured RFQs
  • Collecting and comparing quotations
  • Aligning on delivery times and logistics expectations

Quality & technical coordination

We keep communication with the factory smooth. Technical responsibility remains with the manufacturer and your engineers – we make sure information flows clearly.

  • Coordinating technical submittals and datasheets
  • Supporting FAT arrangements and pre-shipment checks
  • Following up on open points until closure

Meetings, translation & follow-up

Language should never stop a good project. We support you in English, Arabic and German during calls, online meetings and factory visits.

  • Bilingual project calls and online meetings
  • Support at trade fairs and factory visits
  • Structured follow-up notes and action lists

How we work with you

Our process is simple and transparent. You always know what the next step is and who is responsible for what.

  1. 1. Requirements call. We clarify your project scope, target sectors and timelines.
  2. 2. Supplier shortlist. You receive a focused list of suitable German manufacturers.
  3. 3. Introductions. We organise meetings and share the required technical information.
  4. 4. RFQ & offers. We support you with structured enquiries and follow-up on quotations.
  5. 5. Agreement between you and the manufacturer. Commercial and legal agreements are concluded directly between both parties.
  6. 6. Ongoing coordination. We remain available for clarifications and future projects.

Important: We do not provide legal, tax or financial advice. Our role is to help both parties communicate clearly and build sustainable, long-term business relationships.
