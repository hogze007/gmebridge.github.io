Services for Saudi Buyers & Partners

GME Bridge connects Saudi distributors, EPCs and industrial end users with reliable German and European manufacturers. We support you from the first supplier search to successful delivery – always on your side, bilingual and focused on long-term partnerships.

Discuss your project

What We Do

Our services are designed for Saudi companies that want high-quality German technology without the communication gaps and uncertainty.

Representation in Front of German Manufacturers

We act as your local point of contact in Germany. We present your company to suitable manufacturers, explain your project requirements, and make sure both sides understand each other – commercially and technically.

  • Profile your company and target applications
  • Introduce you to relevant German OEMs
  • Support with follow-up and clarifications

Supplier Scouting & Shortlists

Instead of searching the internet for days, you receive a curated shortlist of manufacturers that fit your technical, quality and delivery requirements.

  • Market screening in Germany and Europe
  • Evaluation of certifications, experience and references
  • Summary of key strengths for each manufacturer

Commercial Mediation & Deal Support

We support the communication between your team and the manufacturer during enquiries and negotiations. Final commercial and legal decisions always remain between you and the supplier.

  • Clarifying technical & commercial questions
  • Coordinating revised offers and options
  • Helping both sides reach a clear, workable agreement

Procurement & RFQ Support

From RFQ to purchase order, we keep the process structured and transparent, especially when multiple offers and configurations are involved.

  • Preparing and sending structured RFQs
  • Collecting and comparing quotations
  • Coordinating delivery times and logistics expectations

Quality & Technical Coordination

We help you stay close to the factory. We coordinate documentation and communication so that quality expectations are clear on both sides. Technical responsibility remains with the manufacturer and your engineers.

  • Coordinating technical submittals and datasheets
  • Supporting with FAT arrangements and pre-shipment checks
  • Follow-up on open points until closure

Meetings, Translation & Follow-Up

Language should never stop a good project. We support you in Arabic, English and German during calls, online meetings and factory visits.

  • Bilingual project calls and online meetings
  • Support during trade fairs and factory visits
  • Structured follow-up notes and action lists

How We Work With You

  1. 1. Requirements call. We clarify your project scope, target sectors, and timelines.
  2. 2. Supplier shortlist. You receive a focused list of suitable German manufacturers.
  3. 3. Introductions. We organise meetings and share the required technical information.
  4. 4. RFQ & offers. We support you with structured enquiries and follow-up on quotations.
  5. 5. Agreement between you and the manufacturer. Commercial and legal agreements are concluded directly between both parties.
  6. 6. Ongoing coordination. We stay available for clarifications and future projects.
