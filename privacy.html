Privacy policy

How GME Bridge handles your data.

This page explains in simple terms which personal data may be processed when you visit this website or contact GME Bridge, and for which purposes this happens.

1. Controller

The controller for data processing in the context of this website and email communication is:

GME Bridge
Ratingen, Germany
Email: info@gmebridge.com

2. What data is processed when you visit this website?

a) Technical data & logs

This website is a simple static site hosted on external infrastructure (for example GitHub Pages and DNS via Cloudflare). When you access the site, technical information is automatically processed by the servers, such as:

  • IP address and approximate location (country / region)
  • Date and time of access
  • Requested pages and files
  • Browser type and version, operating system

This technical data is used to deliver the website, to ensure stability and security and, where applicable, for basic, anonymous statistics. We do not use this data to personally identify visitors.

b) Cookies & tracking

The current version of this website does not use personalised tracking cookies, marketing pixels or analytics tools such as Google Analytics. Only technically necessary cookies may be used by the hosting provider to operate the service securely.

3. What data is processed when you contact us?

If you contact GME Bridge by email or via the contact form, we process the information you actively provide, for example:

  • Your name and contact details (such as email address)
  • Company and role (if provided)
  • Content of your message, including project information
  • Any attachments you send (e.g. datasheets, RFQs)

We use this data exclusively to handle your enquiry, to communicate with you and, where relevant, to prepare potential cooperation with German manufacturers.

Legal basis (where applicable under GDPR): Art. 6 (1) lit. b (pre-contractual measures) and lit. f (legitimate interest in structured communication with potential business partners).

4. Do we share data with third parties?

We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising purposes. However, in the context of a concrete project or enquiry, it may be necessary to forward parts of your information to selected manufacturers, for example:

  • To check whether a manufacturer can support your project
  • To prepare or clarify a technical or commercial offer

We only share information that is necessary and relevant for the specific case and, where possible, after clarifying this with you.

5. Where is data stored and for how long?

Emails and related project documents are typically stored in our email system and, where necessary, in structured folders (for example in cloud storage or local devices).

We keep business-related communication for as long as it is necessary for:

  • Handling your enquiry or project
  • Documenting cooperation and follow-up
  • Fulfilling legal retention obligations (where applicable)

If you would like specific information or documents to be deleted earlier, please contact us by email.

6. Your rights (where GDPR applies)

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access (information about data we process about you)
  • Right to rectification (correction of inaccurate data)
  • Right to erasure (“right to be forgotten” in certain cases)
  • Right to restriction of processing
  • Right to data portability (in certain constellations)
  • Right to object to processing based on legitimate interests

To exercise these rights, you can contact us at info@gmebridge.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state of your habitual residence, place of work or the place of the alleged infringement.

7. Changes to this privacy policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time, for example if the website, the services or the legal framework change. The current version is always available on this page.
